# Privacy Policy for Lumen Blocks

**Effective date:** July 28, 2026  
**Last updated:** July 28, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Jdrexx ("we," "us," or "our") handles
information when you use the Lumen Blocks Android application (the "App").

## Summary

Lumen Blocks is designed to work offline. We do not operate an advertising or
analytics service for the App, and we do not sell personal information. Most
game information is stored only on your device.

If Google Play Games features are enabled and you use them, Google may process
your Play Games identity, scores, achievements, and related app or device
information to provide those features.

## Information stored on your device

The App stores gameplay and preference information locally, including:

- high scores and local leaderboard entries;
- game progress, Lumen, restoration stages, and daily progress;
- gameplay statistics such as games played, pieces placed, lines cleared, and
  maximum combo;
- achievements earned within the App; and
- settings such as sound, haptics, tutorial completion, and high-contrast mode.

This information is used to operate the game, remember your progress, and
restore your preferences. We do not receive this locally stored information on
developer-operated servers.

Android or a device backup service may back up and restore App data according
to your device and Google account settings. Those backup services are
controlled by their respective providers.

## Google Play Games Services

The App may offer optional Google Play Games features, including platform
authentication, global leaderboards, and achievements. When these features are
available and used, the App may send Google:

- your game scores and selected game mode;
- achievement status and qualifying gameplay totals; and
- requests to authenticate your Play Games profile or display Google Play
  Games interfaces.

Google Play Games may independently collect or process information such as your
gamer identity or account information, IP address, approximate country or
region, device identifiers, app and device information, game activity,
achievements, and scores. Google processes that information under the
[Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) and your Google
Play Games settings.

We use Google Play Games information only to provide authentication,
leaderboards, achievements, game progress features, service integrity, and
related troubleshooting. We do not receive your Google account password.

You can play the offline portions of Lumen Blocks without using Google Play
Games.

## Information we do not intentionally collect

The App does not intentionally collect or request:

- precise location;
- contacts;
- photos, videos, or personal files;
- camera or microphone recordings;
- health, financial, or payment information;
- advertising identifiers for targeted advertising; or
- messages or browsing history.

The current App does not include third-party advertising or developer-operated
behavioral analytics.

## Sharing a score

If you choose the App's score-sharing feature, Android opens a system sharing
interface. Nothing is shared until you select a destination and confirm the
action. The selected application or service handles the shared content under
its own privacy policy.

## How information is used

Information is used as reasonably necessary to:

- provide and maintain gameplay;
- save progress and settings;
- authenticate players who choose Google Play Games;
- submit and display leaderboard scores and achievements;
- protect the integrity and security of game services; and
- diagnose technical problems.

We do not use App information for targeted advertising, and we do not sell or
rent personal information.

## Data sharing and service providers

We may transmit the limited game information described above to Google when
providing Google Play Games features. Google acts under its own terms and
privacy practices. Information may also be disclosed when required by law, to
protect users or the public, or in connection with a business transfer where
legally permitted.

We do not otherwise share personal information with independent third parties
for their own advertising or marketing purposes.

## Data retention and deletion

Local game information remains on your device until it is overwritten, you
clear the App's storage, or you uninstall the App. A device backup may retain a
copy according to the backup provider's settings and retention practices.

Google retains and deletes information processed by Google Play Games according
to Google's policies and your Google account settings. You can manage or
delete Play Games data through your Google Play Games or Google account
settings.

We do not maintain user accounts or a separate developer database containing
your gameplay information. If you submit a privacy inquiry, we retain the
inquiry only as long as reasonably necessary to respond, maintain appropriate
records, and comply with legal obligations.

## Security

We use reasonable safeguards appropriate to the App's design. Local data is
stored within Android's application storage, subject to Android's security and
backup controls. Communications with Google Play Games are handled through
Google's Android services. No method of storage or transmission is completely
secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

## Children's privacy

Lumen Blocks is intended for a general audience and is not directed to children
under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from
children. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us,
please use the contact mechanism below so we can evaluate and address the
request.

Parents and guardians should review the child-account and parental-control
options provided by Google and the device platform.

## Your privacy choices and rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to request access, correction,
deletion, restriction, or information about the handling of your personal
information. Because we do not operate an App account system or gameplay
database, we ordinarily cannot identify or retrieve data stored only on your
device or held by Google.

You can:

- clear local data through Android's App Info settings;
- uninstall the App;
- manage Android backup settings;
- manage Play Games privacy, sign-in, and profile settings through Google; and
- request deletion of Play Games data through your Google account controls.

We will not discriminate against you for exercising applicable privacy rights.

## International processing

Google and other platform providers may process information in countries other
than the country where you live. Their privacy policies describe the safeguards
they use for international processing.

## Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the App, its services, or legal
requirements change. We will update the "Last updated" date and publish the
revised policy at the same location. Material changes may also be communicated
through the App or its store listing when appropriate.
